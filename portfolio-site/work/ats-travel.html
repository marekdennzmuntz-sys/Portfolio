01 / The brief

ATS Travel had considerable operational credibility, but that strength was not yet translated into a coherent advocacy base. The campaign needed to mobilise clients, staff, and industry partners around an award vote without sacrificing trust or sounding like an empty popularity drive.

Strategic opportunity

Position ATS Travel as Kenya's leading travel management company by turning everyday relationships into a voluntary, informed base of support — and then use the award moment to strengthen longer-term B2B credibility.

02 / The system

Three campaign moments, designed as one system.

01

Relationship cultivation

Build the trust that makes advocacy credible before an ask is made.

  • Re-engaged corporate clients with value-led insights
  • Strengthened airline, hotel, and DMC partnerships
  • Shared micro-stories of travel success to retain relationship texture
02

Vote activation

Give internal teams and external partners useful, brand-consistent ways to participate.

  • Created team briefings and pride-building content
  • Built voting kits, shareable assets, and outreach scripts
  • Positioned ATS as a national contender via LinkedIn thought leadership
  • Sequenced a four-week social outreach with direct calls to action
03

Prestige + reveal

Convert the result into a credible commercial story rather than a one-day announcement.

  • Directed a high-touch award announcement video
  • Rolled out branded reveal assets across channels
  • Crafted client-appreciation and press-style messaging
  • Extended the win into 2025 business-travel positioning
03 / Outcomes

What moved.

The campaign secured the 2025 World Travel Award for Kenya's Leading Travel Management Company while improving partner and client engagement. The strategic intent was to let the result function as an endorsement of a more mature, relationship-led brand — not merely a trophy announcement.
