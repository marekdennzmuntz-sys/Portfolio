01 / The brief

A.Nanjala Careers needed to arrive as a premium resource for ambitious professionals without relying on the fragmented visual language and generic career copy that often flatten the category. The launch had to express credibility, ambition, and practical value across early digital touchpoints.

Strategic opportunity

Build an identity and launch system that helps the brand make a stronger first impression, create a consistent user-facing voice, and turn early attention into consultation interest.

02 / The system

One identity system, built for every first impression.

01

Set the position

Establish a premium, relevant personality before production begins.

  • Clarified the audience: ambitious professionals across Africa
  • Shaped the visual and verbal principles for launch
  • Defined the tone behind the brand's initial communications
02

Build the working kit

Create a practical toolkit that makes consistency possible, not aspirational.

  • Developed the brand identity kit
  • Created social-media template direction
  • Designed a caption and content system
  • Produced landing-page writing for the initial experience
03

Make the launch useful

Use the visual and content system to create a clear, credible market entry.

  • Directed launch content across relevant social touchpoints
  • Connected visual language to professional audience needs
  • Supported organic consultation interest through clearer calls to action
03 / Outcomes

What moved.

The launch established a more cohesive digital presence and generated early visibility, engagement, and organic consultation interest. The value of the work was the system itself: a usable foundation that allowed the brand to show up with greater clarity and consistency.
